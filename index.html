Why testing hundreds of features needs a smarter bar than 5%

A p-value of 0.05 is fine for one test, but screen 500 features and roughly 25 will look significant by chance. Controlling the false discovery rate with Benjamini-Hochberg keeps the shortlist honest.

  • Ask what proportion of your shortlist is expected to be noise, not just whether a feature is significant
  • Leaner models, less overfitting and easier drift management
  • It filters noise but does not fix multicollinearity, so pair it with an effect-size check

27 Sep 2026

Your model's accuracy has a personality

A single AUC of 0.83 is a snapshot, not the whole story. Give your metric a confidence interval instead, and you can see how much wiggle room to expect on a different day.

  • Before celebrating a new model, check whether the two ranges overlap
  • A very wide interval is the model being upfront about having less to go on
  • Bootstrap resampling gets you there: resample, recompute, and read the middle 95%
